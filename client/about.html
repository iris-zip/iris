About Iriszip

Iriszip started from a simple frustration: to send someone a file, you usually have to upload it to a company first — and trust that company with whatever's inside.

Most "free" transfer services aren't really free. They pay for themselves by holding onto your data, scanning it, or building a profile around it. Iriszip is built the opposite way. It doesn't hold anything. It encrypts your file on your device, connects you to the person you're sending to, and gets out of the way.

It's an independent project — not a startup chasing your data, just a tool that does one thing and respects you while doing it. Built and maintained in Georgia.

If Iriszip is useful to you, the best way to keep it running is to support it.
