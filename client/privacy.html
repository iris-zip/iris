Privacy

Most privacy pages are long because they have a lot to hide. This one is short.

We don't store your files. We don't log what's in them. We can't read them — they're encrypted on your device before they're sent, and only the person you're sending to can open them.

We don't track you. No ads, no advertising cookies, no third-party trackers following you around. The only information we handle is the bare minimum needed to connect two devices for a transfer, and it isn't used to identify you.

That's it. If anything here is ever unclear, just ask.
