Iriszip moves files and messages between two devices and keeps nothing. These terms say what that means for you and for us. They apply to the service at iriszip.com; the open-source code has its own licenses.

What the service is

You pair two devices with a code, and Iriszip carries encrypted data between them — directly when it can, through our relay when it can't. A session lasts up to 60 minutes and a file can be up to 1 GB. Nothing is stored: when a session ends, the transfer is gone from our side. Iriszip is a transfer, not a place to keep things — if you need a copy later, keep your own.
