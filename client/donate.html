Keep Iris free.

Iriszip is free to use, with no account and nothing stored. There is no data to sell and nothing to advertise — so it stays alive on donations.

Real things cost money: the servers, the relay that carries files across restrictive networks, the domain, and an independent security audit. If Iriszip has saved you from a paid transfer service even once, a small contribution goes a long way.

Where your support goes

  • Infrastructure — the signalling server, the domain, and the certificates behind them.
  • Relay servers — the relay that carries a transfer when a network refuses a direct path between two devices, which mobile carriers make common.
  • Security research — independent cryptanalysis of the protocol, and the work of fixing what it finds.
  • Development — keeping Iriszip working across browsers, phones and networks that change under it.
